Allow Baplet to open

Your browser may ask to open System Settings. Allow it, and Privacy & Security opens.

Open Privacy & Security

  1. Open Baplet once from your Applications folder first. macOS blocks it and says it can't verify the developer: click Done.
  2. In Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Baplet and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. Baplet opens, and you never have to do this again.

The Open Anyway button only appears after you've tried to open Baplet once. More help: installing Baplet.
